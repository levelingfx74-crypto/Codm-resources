Game modes

Quick explanations and useful focus points.

OBJECTIVE

Hardpoint

Control the active zone, rotate before it changes and support teammates instead of chasing eliminations.

OBJECTIVE

Domination

Capture and hold objectives. Coordinate control of two flags and avoid overextending.

ROUND-BASED

Search & Destroy

One life per round. Communicate, play patiently and keep track of the objective.

ELIMINATION

Team Deathmatch

Work with your team, use cover and avoid giving the other side easy repeat encounters.

ELIMINATION

Frontline

Teams respawn at set bases. Use safe routes out of spawn and stay aware of lane pressure.

Loot, choose a sensible rotation, manage resources and adapt to the safe zone.
